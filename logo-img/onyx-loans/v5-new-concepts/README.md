Same hex symbol (split, lifted, pen nib). New ways to carry "LOANS":

1. **Installment track** (`onyx-new-track-*`): seven slanted segments under the name, gaps = hex gaps, rising left to right. Each segment is a repayment; onyx first (the borrower's start), green as it climbs. Says installment loans + credit growth.
2. **Crystal tag** (`onyx-new-tag-*`): LOANS knocked out of a stretched, slanted hexagon with the symbol's 60° ends. The left point meets ONYX, the right point is the forward arrow. Ownable shape, not the GoLoans parallelogram.
