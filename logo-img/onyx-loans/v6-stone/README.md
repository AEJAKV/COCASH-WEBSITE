# Onyx Loans — v6 "The Stone" (boss direction: juxtapose rough onyx with a gem inside)

Juxtaposition: rough, matte onyx outside / polished, faceted GoLoans-green value inside, revealed by a clean cut across the top.
Wordmark simplified: ONYX in onyx, LOANS in GoLoans green — no effects.

1. **Geode cut** (`onyx-stone-geode-*`): the cut shows onyx's real banded layers with a green brilliant-cut gem at the heart.
2. **Emerging gem** (`onyx-stone-emerge-*`): a cut green gem rises out of the stone, leaning forward.
3. **Lifted lid** (`onyx-stone-lid-*`): the top lifts up and forward, revealing polished green inside.

Regenerate: `python3 scripts/generate-onyx-stone.py OUT_DIR FONT_DIR`.
